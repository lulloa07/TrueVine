🌿 Sensory Processing Inventory

Please describe his/her sensory profile. Is s/he sensory-seeking or sensory-defensive, and for which senses? Consider visual, auditory, tactile, taste/smell, movement (vestibular), heavy work (proprioception), and interoception (sensing internal states, e.g. feeling "full" after a meal).
