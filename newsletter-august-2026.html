Major Milestone

We Are Officially a 501(c)(3).

✦

Our tax-exempt determination has arrived.

After months of preparation, paperwork, and prayer, True Vine Mission has officially received our 501(c)(3) tax-exempt determination letter from the IRS. What began as an application submitted in faith is now a legal reality.

This status is more than a formality. It is a door opening — to grant funding, to institutional partnerships, and to a deeper level of trust with the families and donors who are considering joining us on this journey. It tells the world what we have known all along: this school is not a someday dream. It is being built, one approved step at a time.

We are overwhelmed with gratitude for everyone who prayed us through this process. This one belongs to all of you — and to every student whose voice is waiting to be heard.

Welcome to the Board

Introducing Leslee Dirnberger — Board Member.

✦

We are thrilled to welcome Leslee Dirnberger, JD to the True Vine Mission Board of Directors. Leslee is a nationally recognized leader in alternative K–12 education and child development, and the Founder of Aspire Education. With more than 20 years of experience, she has spent her career empowering families to build individualized educational paths, with a special focus on neurodiverse students.

Leslee Dirnberger, JD

Board Member

A mother of four homeschooled children, Leslee has supported thousands of students throughout her career and is currently training as a Certified Spelling Practitioner through The Spellers Method. Her passion is simple and deeply personal: ensuring every nonspeaking student has the opportunity to communicate, learn, and be heard.

Leslee holds a bachelor's degree from William & Mary and a Juris Doctor from Washington University School of Law — a background that brings invaluable legal and educational insight to our board as we continue building this school.

“I am excited to be a part of True Vine Mission where we can all live out 1 Peter 4:10, ‘As every man hath received grace, ministering the same one to another: as good stewards of the manifold grace of God.’ This learning community is truly a gift from God and I am honored to be a part of the journey!”— Leslee Dirnberger, Board Member

Leslee's heart for neurodiverse learners, combined with her decades of leadership experience, make her an incredible addition to this team. We are so grateful God brought her to True Vine at exactly this season.

Giving Day

About the School

We Are Building Something Different.

Opening February 2027 · Enrollment Inquiries Welcome

True Vine Mission is a faith-based nonprofit school in Celina, Texas dedicated to serving nonspeaking and minimally speaking students through the Spellers Method — a communication approach rooted in presumed competence.
