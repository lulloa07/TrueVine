True Vine Mission is a nonprofit school in development in the DFW area, built for nonspeaking and minimally speaking students. Everything here starts from one belief: every student is capable and worthy of a full education.

This folder holds the story of where the mission is headed, the materials built so far, and the ways to be part of it — browse whatever's most useful to you.
