Certification Milestone

Sunny & Leslee Are Now Certified Spellers Method Providers.

✦

Two members of our team have completed their Spellers Method certification.

We are so proud to share that Sunny Talbert and Leslee Dirnberger, JD have completed the course to become certified Spellers Providers through the Spellers Method program. This is hard, dedicated work, and both of them poured their hearts into it.

Sunny Talbert

Co-Founder · Certified Spellers Method Provider

Leslee Dirnberger, JD

Board Member · Certified Spellers Method Provider

Their certification means our students will be supported from day one by trained providers who presume competence and know how to help a nonspeaking learner find their voice, one letter at a time. Please join us in congratulating them both!

Together in Person

Our Team's First In-Person Meeting.

✦

This fall, the True Vine Mission team gathered for our very first in-person meeting in McKinney, Texas — and what an amazing time it was! After months of calls and video meetings, sitting around the same table to pray, dream, and plan together was a true gift.

Our focus was the opening of our pilot program in February 2027. Every conversation brought us closer to the day we welcome our very first students and families through the doors.

About the School

We Are Building Something Different.

Opening February 2027 · Enrollment Inquiries Welcome

True Vine Mission is a faith-based nonprofit school in Celina, Texas dedicated to serving nonspeaking and minimally speaking students through the Spellers Method — a communication approach rooted in presumed competence.

“Abide in me, and I in you.” — John 15:4

If you feel led to support the mission of True Vine Mission, every gift — large or small — goes directly toward opening our doors and unlocking the voices of our students.
