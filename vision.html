Our Vision

A Place Where Every Voice Matters

The vision behind True Vine Mission, in the founders' own words.

True Vine Mission exists to change how nonspeaking and minimally speaking individuals are educated and cared for in North Texas — by building a model that's professional in instruction and founded on one conviction: every student is capable, and worthy of a full education.

The school was born out of a shared experience among three mothers, who saw what became possible once their children were finally presumed competent. That principle — Presume Competence — is the foundation everything else is built on.

Every person has a purpose. Every voice has value. Every life can bear fruit.
